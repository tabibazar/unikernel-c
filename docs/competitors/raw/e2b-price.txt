
E2B.dev agent sandboxes operate on a freemium model with a "Hobby" tier (free) and paid "Pro" ($150/month) and "Ultimate/Enterprise" tiers, all with additional usage costs. Usage is billed per second. The smallest instance configurable includes 1 vCPU ($0.000014/second) and 512 MiB of RAM ($0.0000045/GiB/second). The Hobby tier includes 10 GiB of storage, while the Pro tier includes 20 GiB. E2B sandboxes have a measured cold-start time of 717ms to create a new sandbox and 662ms to resume a paused one; these are independent measurements, not marketing claims.
