
The HaLVM (Haskell Lightweight Virtual Machine) unikernel is used for building lightweight services in the cloud, particularly web services, by compiling necessary operating system components into a single binary that runs directly on Xen. This approach offers advantages in terms of size, flexibility, and security. Adam Wick, the creator of HaLVM, has led projects at Galois, Inc. that employed HaLVM for communications and network security.
