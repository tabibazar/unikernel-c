Two boots of the same binary on the same host: 679 and 651 mismatches out of
20,000,000, i.e. 3.40e-05 and 3.26e-05 per iteration. The counts differ, and the
first bad iteration differs by a factor of 44 (62,929 vs 1,414) -- which is the
signature of an interrupt-timing fault rather than a deterministic miscomputation.

NOT directly comparable to the 6.35e-07 in ../arithmetic-fault/results.txt.
That figure counts bad MODULO OPERATIONS; this one counts bad ITERATIONS, and
one iteration is a full modular exponentiation, i.e. on the order of a hundred
mulmods. Divided through, the two are the same order of magnitude. The paired
after-run must use the same probe to be meaningful.
