# First 4-term chain

    m = 1,854,182,119
    N_i = m * 2357# * 2^(1+i) - 1,  i = 0..3
    1010 digits each

Found by the local hunt. Independently re-verified at **256 Miller-Rabin reps**
by `verify.c`, which rebuilds the primorial from scratch and constructs each
term directly rather than sharing any code with `cc_hunt`:

```
  N_0 (1010 digits): probably prime
  N_1 (1010 digits): probably prime
  N_2 (1010 digits): probably prime
  N_3 (1010 digits): probably prime
  N_4 (1011 digits): COMPOSITE
```

Four terms, and the chain provably stops there — N_4 is composite, so this is
not a truncated 5 or 6.

**This is not a record and not close to one.** The target is 6. It is the first
result above length 3 in 3.28 billion multipliers, and at the measured ~63x
falloff per term one 4-chain in this much work is roughly what to expect.

It is still a probable-prime chain: `mpz_probab_prime_p` is Miller-Rabin plus
Baillie-PSW. Nothing here would be claimed anywhere without a primality proof,
and at four terms there is nothing worth claiming.
